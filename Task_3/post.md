Task 3 Completed: Exploratory Data Visualization ✅

As part of my Machine Learning Internship at CDAM – Chuka University, I’ve advanced to the third stage of our project: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) through visualization.

This stage involved creating and interpreting multiple plots:

📊 Histogram – to understand variable distributions

📦 Boxplot – to detect outliers and spread

🔹 Scatter Plot – to explore relationships between variables

📈 Bar Chart & Pie Chart – to summarize categorical data

🌡️ Correlation Heatmap – to identify strong feature relationships

🔗 Pair Plot – to visualize multi‑variable interactions

🎻 Violin Plot – to combine distribution and density insights

For each visualization, I explained why it was selected, interpreted the findings, and discussed unusual observations (like skewness or outliers).

✨ What I Learnt in the Process:

How different plots reveal unique aspects of the data (distribution, relationships, anomalies).

The importance of choosing the right visualization to avoid misleading interpretations.

How outliers and skewness can significantly affect model performance if not addressed early.

That visualization is not just about aesthetics — it’s about storytelling and uncovering actionable insights.

📊 Impact: Visualization transforms numbers into stories. By uncovering hidden patterns and anomalies, we gain clarity on which features drive outcomes — setting the stage for feature engineering and model building.

Excited to move into the next phase: model development and evaluation!
