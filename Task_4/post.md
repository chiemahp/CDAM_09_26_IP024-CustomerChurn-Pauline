Task 4 Completed: Machine Learning ✅

As part of my Machine Learning Internship at CDAM – Chuka University, I’ve completed the fourth stage of our project: model development and evaluation.

This stage involved:

Loading the dataset and inspecting its structure

Splitting data into training and testing sets

Training 5 different ML models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Decision Tree, SVM, and Gradient Boosting)

Tuning hyperparameters to optimize performance

Evaluating and comparing models using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1‑score

Selecting the best performing model and summarizing the results in a comparison table

✨ What I Learnt in the Process:

Different models capture patterns differently — tuning hyperparameters can significantly shift performance.

Evaluation metrics must be chosen carefully; accuracy alone can be misleading in imbalanced datasets.

Comparing models side‑by‑side builds confidence in selecting the most reliable solution.

The importance of reproducibility — documenting experiments ensures results can be validated and scaled.

📊 Impact: This step transforms cleaned and wrangled data into predictive power. By selecting the best model, we move closer to actionable insights that can guide decision‑making in real‑world scenarios.
