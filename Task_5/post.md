Task 5 Completed: Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) ✅

As part of my Machine Learning Internship at CDAM – Chuka University, I’ve completed the fifth stage of our project: bringing transparency to machine learning models through Explainable AI (XAI).

This stage involved:

Loading the trained ML model and test dataset

Creating explainer objects using SHAP, LIME, and DALEX

Evaluating global feature importance to identify key drivers of predictions

Generating Partial Dependence Plots (PDP) and Accumulated Local Effects (ALE) for selected predictors

Explaining individual predictions using SHAP values and Break Down plots

Interpreting results and discussing how predictors influence model decisions

Presenting a concise summary of findings with recommendations for decision‑making

✨ What I Learnt in the Process:

Transparency builds trust — XAI helps stakeholders understand why a model predicts a certain outcome.

Global feature importance highlights the most influential variables, while local explanations show how they impact individual cases.

Visual tools like PDPs and SHAP plots make complex ML logic accessible to non‑technical audiences.

Explainability is not just a technical add‑on — it’s essential for ethical, reliable, and actionable AI solutions.

📊 Impact: By integrating XAI, our project ensures that predictions are not only accurate but also interpretable. This empowers decision‑makers to act confidently on model outputs, bridging the gap between data science and real‑world strategy.

Excited to move into the final stage: deployment, where these insights will be delivered through a user‑friendly application!
